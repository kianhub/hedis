## Follow-up (post-burn)

We will tune a Go **coverage check** with threshold **0.40** (calibrated **without** v6) to emit **`status=unsupported_bytecode`** instead of crashing when fingerprint coverage is insufficient.

## Interpretation

Held-out exact F1 near **0.47** remains below engineering targets, but vulnerability-aligned **0.883** suggests the matcher surfaces security-relevant packages when versions exist in the DB. **zeus** demonstrates that fallback cannot rescue apps whose bytecode hash space is nearly disjoint from the reference corpus.
