## Interpretation and limits

**While** control-group exact F1 remained **0.703** for both M37 and M38, fill-group F1 rose **0.346 → 0.549**. This pattern supports the hypothesis that the aggregate gain is **localized to fill-target RN versions**, not to a shift in application mix on the control slice.

**However**, the fill improvement rests primarily on **two** RN **0.72** applications (**jitsi**, **daimo**); **gsy** (0.80) was already high at **0.875**, and **xaman** remained at **0** for coverage reasons. We **cannot** generalize the fill effect to RN **0.75** or **0.78** without a v8 panel that includes those versions.

M38 simultaneously changes the **database pin**, the **matcher binary**, and the **coverage gate**. We did **not** ablate these components on v7. Isolating them would require development-split experiments or a **fresh v8** freeze; **rescoring v7 is not permitted**.
