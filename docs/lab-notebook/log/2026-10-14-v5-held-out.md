## Interpretation

Precision exceeded recall on the full twelve-app panel, but the crash rate dominated the denominator. **While** the six successful apps approached usable vulnerability F1, **we cannot** treat the aggregate **0.482** exact F1 as a fair ceiling until RN **0.72/0.75/0.78/0.80** fingerprints exist.
